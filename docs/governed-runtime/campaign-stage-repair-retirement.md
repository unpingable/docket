# Campaign-stage repair retirement

The campaign-stage repair office is historical. Docket retains its enum tags,
row decoding, and canonical artifact parser only to inspect old evidence with
its original identity. Archive parsing establishes byte and transcript
integrity; it does not establish current state correspondence or authority.

Current code refuses all of the former live entries:

- `records_repair_stage` and `existing_source_scope_repair_stage` proposal
  admission;
- `exact_repair` adjudication;
- campaign-stage repair standing issuance or consumption;
- repair-authority issue, export, and verification;
- direct persistence of newly fabricated legacy proposals, standing, or
  adjudications.

Ordinary operator and reviewer campaign stages remain available. Governed
repair is a separate custody protocol with its own exact request, decision,
grant, acceptance, and one-use completion lineage. There is no conversion from
a historical campaign-stage repair record or artifact into that protocol.

NQ C1 fixtures under `conformance/campaign/nq-c1/` are immutable historical
specimens. They do not bootstrap standing, custody, qualification, or
certificate authority and do not imply that Docket governed the recorded
historical occurrences.
